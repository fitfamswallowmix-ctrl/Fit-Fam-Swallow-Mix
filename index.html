Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How do I prepare Fit-Fam Swallow Mix?
Bring water to a rolling boil in a pot. Gradually pour in the Fit-Fam Swallow Mix while stirring continuously to prevent lumps. Stir firmly until a smooth, stretchable dough forms. Cover and allow to steam on low heat for 2-3 minutes, then turn once more before serving.
How long does the pack last?
Unopened packs have a shelf life of up to 12 months when stored in a cool, dry place. Once opened, seal tightly and consume within 6-8 weeks for optimal freshness.
How should I store the mix?
Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture. Ensure the resealable pouch is zip-locked tight after each use.
